## 4. How We Work

**Eyebrow:** How We Work

## How an opportunity moves from potential to execution

*Image:* A real-estate team reviewing development plans and a site model (`images/sandcorp-opportunity-team.webp`)
*Caption:* Every stage tested before the next begins.

1. **Opportunity** — A potential opportunity enters the SandCorp network.
2. **Diagnosis** — Understand the opportunity, where the value lies and what is preventing progress.
3. **Alignment** — Identify the parties, capabilities and resources required.
4. **Structuring** — Develop the commercial and partnership architecture required.
5. **Decision** — Test the opportunity against its commercial, technical and strategic realities.
6. **Execution** — Where viable, enable the relevant parties to move forward, with SandCorp participating according to its role.

## 5. Where We Create Value

**Eyebrow:** Where We Create Value

## Five points where structure changes the outcome

Value is rarely missing. More often it is unassembled, untested or unstructured. We work at the points where the right structure turns latent value into an opportunity that can be funded, delivered and shared.

*Image:* A landscaped mixed-use district connecting established buildings and new development (`images/sandcorp-value-district.webp`)
*Caption:* From latent value to viable opportunity

- **Opportunity Origination** — Finding and assembling opportunities before they are investable.
- **Feasibility & Diligence** — Testing whether an opportunity is commercially and technically sound.
- **Transaction Structuring** — Building the legal and commercial architecture that carries a deal to execution.
- **Partnership Formation** — Aligning the parties, roles and governance a structure needs to hold.
- **Participation** — SandCorp holds a stake in the value it helps create, where appropriate.
